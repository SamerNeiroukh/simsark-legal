In short

SIMSARK (سمسارك) helps people list and find apartments in Jerusalem and the surrounding area. We provide the noticeboard. We are not the agent, the landlord, the seller, or a party to any deal you make.

Post honestly, deal in good faith, and be careful — the same care you would take with any classified listing.

SIMSARK is operated by Samer Neiroukh. Contact: simsark.support@gmail.com

2. Using SIMSARK

You may use SIMSARK if you are 18 or older and able to enter a contract.

You need an account to post a listing, send messages, save favourites, or report a listing. You can browse without one.

By creating an account you agree to these terms.

You are responsible for your account, including keeping your password to yourself. Tell us promptly if you think someone else is using it.

One person, one account. Do not create accounts to evade a restriction we have placed on you.

3. What SIMSARK is — and is not

SIMSARK is a noticeboard. Users post listings; other users read them and get in touch through messages in the app.

We do not:

  • verify that a listing is genuine, accurate, available, or lawfully offered
  • verify that a person has the right to rent or sell a property
  • inspect properties
  • take part in, guarantee, or hold money for any transaction
  • act as an agent or broker for anyone

Any agreement you reach is between you and the other person. We are not a party to it and are not responsible for it.

This is the most important term in this document. Please treat listings the way you would treat any classified advertisement: verify independently, visit in person, and be cautious with money.

4. Posting a listing

By posting, you confirm that:

  • You have the right to offer this property, as owner or with the owner's permission
  • The details are accurate and not misleading — price, size, location, condition
  • The photos are of this property and you have the right to use them
  • The property is genuinely available on the terms stated
  • Your listing complies with the law that applies to you

Every new listing, and every change to one, is reviewed by a moderator before it is published. We may decline to publish a listing that breaks these terms.

You keep ownership of what you post. You give us permission to host, display, resize, and distribute it within SIMSARK so that the service can function. This permission ends when you delete the content, except for copies already made for backups or that we must keep by law.

Your listing is public

Your listing — including your name — is visible to anyone, including people who are not signed in. Your phone number is not shown. Do not include anything, in the text or the photos, that you are not willing to make public.

You choose how precisely the address is shown. If you choose not to show it exactly, the exact address is kept access-controlled and is not shown to people browsing. See the Privacy Policy.

5. What you must not do

We have no tolerance for objectionable content or abusive users. This applies to listings, photos, profile details and messages alike.

Do not:

  • Post a listing for a property that does not exist, is not available, or is not yours to offer
  • Post false, misleading, or deliberately incomplete information, including a price you do not intend to honour
  • Post someone else's photos, text, or property details without permission
  • Post another person's personal information
  • Post or send content that is unlawful, hateful, harassing, threatening, violent, or sexually explicit
  • Use messages to harass, threaten, spam, or defraud anyone, or to ask for payment outside a genuine deal
  • Discriminate in a listing or in dealing with users, including on the basis of race, religion, national origin, gender, disability, or family status
  • Use SIMSARK to advertise anything other than property
  • Collect, scrape, or bulk-extract listings or contact details, by any means, automated or manual
  • Attempt to gain access to data that is not yours, probe or test our security, or interfere with the service
  • Impersonate anyone, or misrepresent your connection to a property

Scraping and bulk collection are called out because they are the abuse this kind of service attracts, and because they harm the users whose details are taken.

6. Reporting, blocking and moderation

If a listing breaks these terms, report it from the listing. If a message does, report it from the conversation. You can block any user from a conversation; they can then no longer message you.

We may remove a listing, restrict an account, or refuse service, where we believe these terms have been broken or a listing puts users at risk. Where we can, we will say why.

We review reports within 24 hours, and remove content and restrict the accounts responsible where these terms have been broken. Reporting something does not guarantee it will be removed.

If you think we got it wrong, email simsark.support@gmail.com.

7. Your account and content

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account security → Delete account. This removes your profile, listings, and photos, and cannot be undone. Messages you sent stay in the other person's copy of the conversation, with your name removed — see the Privacy Policy.

We may suspend or close an account that repeatedly or seriously breaks these terms. Where the breach is not serious, we will normally warn first.
