Delete your SIMSARK account

This page explains how to delete your account in the SIMSARK app (سمسارك), operated by Samer Neiroukh, and what happens to your data.

In the app

  1. Open Profile.
  2. Tap Account security.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm with your email address.

For your safety you need to have signed in recently. If the app asks, sign in again and repeat the steps. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

If you cannot use the app

Email simsark.support@gmail.com from the email address on your account, with the subject Delete my account. We will confirm it is you, then delete the account within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, phone number, profile photo
  • Your listings and their photos
  • Your saved listings
  • Reports you submitted
  • Photos you sent in messages
  • Your client records, if you used the realtor tools
  • Your sign-in account

What is kept, and for how long

  • Messages you sent stay in the other person's copy of the conversation, with your name removed, until the conversation expires — 12 months after its last message. We cannot delete someone else's record of a conversation they took part in.
  • Messages someone reported, and our decision on them, are kept for 2 years as a record of moderation.
  • Technical error reports are not linked to your account and expire on their own.

The full details are in our privacy policy.
