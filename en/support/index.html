Help & support

We are a small team and we read every message.

Contact us

Email samer.neiroukh@gmail.com. We answer within two working days.

Write from the email address on your account if your question is about your account, so we can find it. Never send us your password.

Report a listing or a message

You do not need to email us for this:

  • A listing: open it, tap the menu at the top, and choose Report listing.
  • A message: in the conversation, press and hold the message and choose Report. You can also block the person from the conversation menu; they will no longer be able to message you.

We review reports within 24 hours.

Stay safe

  • Visit a property in person before paying anything.
  • Never send a deposit to someone you have not met, or to a different name from the one on the listing.
  • Keep the conversation in SIMSARK until you are sure. We cannot help with conversations that happened elsewhere.
