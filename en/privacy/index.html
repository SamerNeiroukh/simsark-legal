Last updated: 31 October 2026

In short

SIMSARK (سمسارك) is a marketplace for apartments in Jerusalem and the surrounding area. To use it you give us an account and — if you post a listing — details about the property. People get in touch through messages inside the app.

The short version of what matters:

  • Your listing is public. Your name and your listing's photos and description can be seen by anyone, including people who are not signed in.
  • Your phone number is not public. People contact you through in-app messages, so nobody needs your number to reach you.
  • Your exact address is not public. If you choose to hide it, it is held separately from the listing and is not sent to anyone browsing the app.
  • We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your account from inside the app, and it really deletes.

The rest of this document is the precise version. Where the two disagree, the precise version is the one that counts.

Address visibility — read this part

When you post a listing you choose how precisely its location is shown:

  • Exact — the address is shown on the listing.
  • Approximate — only the neighbourhood is shown.

If you choose anything other than exact, your exact address and coordinates are stored in a separate, access-controlled place and are not sent to people browsing the app. This is enforced by the database, not by hiding a field in the app. Only you can retrieve them.

We are describing this in detail because it is a promise about how the system actually behaves, not a statement of intent.

Your device's location

If you allow it, the app reads your device's approximate location once, to pick the area nearest to you and show listings there first. The location is used on your device and is not sent to us or stored. You can refuse, or turn it off later in your phone's settings, and choose an area by hand instead.

Photos you upload

Before any photo leaves your phone — for a listing, your profile, or a message — the app re-encodes it and removes its hidden metadata, including the location where it was taken and the camera details. Only the picture itself is uploaded.

If you use the realtor tools

Accounts registered as realtors can keep a private list of their clients: a name, what the client is looking for, a budget, follow-up dates and notes. Only the realtor who wrote them can see these records. If you add someone to that list, you are responsible for having a reason to keep their details and for telling them. The records are deleted when the realtor deletes them or deletes their account.

Kept on your device, not sent to us

Some things stay on your phone and are never uploaded:

  • Listings you are part-way through writing (drafts), including any address you have typed but not yet posted
  • The listings you recently looked at
  • Your language, your selected city, and whether you have seen the intro screens
  • Your sign-in session

In the mobile app, your sign-in session is stored using your device's own secure storage — the Keychain on iPhone, the Keystore on Android. It is excluded from Android backup and device transfer, and on iPhone it is not copied to iCloud.

Listing drafts and unsent messages are kept in the same secure storage, and expire after 30 days. The listings you recently viewed and your preferences are ordinary app data: they stay on the device and are never uploaded, but they are not held in secure storage.

If you delete the app, your drafts, recent listings and preferences go with it. On iPhone, the stored sign-in session is kept by the system rather than by the app, so it is not erased at the moment you delete — it stays encrypted, no other app can read it, and it is discarded if you install SIMSARK again. Deleting your account, or signing out before you delete the app, removes it immediately.

Technical error reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, we receive a report describing what went wrong — the type of error, where in the app it happened, and technical details about the device and app version. This is used only to find and fix problems.

We use Sentry for this. Error reports are only sent from non-development builds — the versions distributed through the app stores.

Messages you send in the app

Messages are the only way users contact each other in SIMSARK — you can talk to the person who posted a listing without either of you sharing a phone number. When you do, we store:

  • the text of the messages, and any photos you attach to them
  • who sent each message and when
  • which listing the conversation is about
  • whether a message has been delivered to the other person's device and whether they have opened the conversation
  • who you have blocked, and any message you report to us

Both people in a conversation can read everything in it. That is the point of a conversation, but it is worth saying plainly: a message is not private from the person you sent it to, and they can screenshot or copy it.

Photos you attach are stored privately. They are not published, not attached to any listing, and are only reachable by the two people in the conversation through short-lived links the app generates as it displays them.

We do not read your conversations, with one exception: if someone reports a message, a moderator sees that message, both display names, and the reason it was reported, so the report can be judged. They do not see the rest of the conversation. Every moderation decision is recorded.

We keep an entry in your notification list when a message arrives. It stores which conversation it belongs to and nothing else — the text you see there is read from the message itself, so deleting a message removes it from the notification too.

If you delete your account, your messages stay in the other person's copy of the conversation, with your name removed. We cannot delete their copy without destroying their record of a conversation they took part in — the same way deleting your email account does not empty other people's inboxes. The photos you sent are deleted.

How long we keep things

Messaging data has a defined life, enforced by a job that runs every night:

What How long
A conversation and its messages 12 months after the last message in it
Photos sent in a conversation 6 months, then deleted; the text stays
Messages you report to us, and our decision 2 years
Entries in your notification list 90 days
People you have blocked While your account exists

Twelve months because a conversation here is a record of a deal — a price discussed, a viewing arranged — and that is how long a dispute about one can reasonably take to surface. A block does not expire, because a block that quietly lapses is a safety problem, not a privacy improvement.

A precise retention schedule is still being defined. Until it is published here, the rule we apply is: data is kept while it is needed for the purpose it was collected for, and deleted when it is not.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from Profile → Account security → Delete account inside the app. The same instructions, including what to do if you cannot sign in, are at the account-deletion page linked from the app and from our store listings.

Deletion removes your profile, your listings, your listing photos, your favourites, your reports, and your sign-in identity. It cannot be undone.

For your safety, you must have signed in recently before you can delete — this is to make sure it is really you.

If you cannot sign in and want your account deleted, email samer.neiroukh@gmail.com from the address on the account. We will verify that it is you before doing anything.

Two honest notes:

  • Technical error reports already sent to Sentry are not linked to your account and are not removed by account deletion. They expire on their own.
  • If deletion is interrupted, it can be resumed by signing in and trying again. If it will not complete, email us.

Your choices

  • See and change your data — your profile and listings are editable in the app at any time.
  • Control your address — choose the visibility when posting, and change it later by editing the listing.
  • Delete — as above.
  • Ask us — email samer.neiroukh@gmail.com with any request about your data, including a copy of it. We will respond.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, tell us first — we would rather fix it. You may also complain to a data protection authority where one has jurisdiction over you.

Children

SIMSARK is not intended for people under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, email samer.neiroukh@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We protect your data with, among other things: encrypted connections; a database that enforces who may read which rows and which columns rather than relying on the app to hide them; separate storage for exact addresses; and, in the mobile app, your device's own secure storage for your sign-in session.

No service can promise perfect security, and we will not pretend otherwise. What we can promise is that when we find a weakness we fix it and, where it affected you, tell you.

Planned — not active yet
